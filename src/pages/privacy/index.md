## If you use Plait through a care provider

If you use Plait as a care worker, client, family member or member of office staff, your care provider decides what data is held about you and why. They are the **data controller**, and their own privacy notice applies. We are their **data processor**: we host and process that data only on their instructions and under a contract with them.

To ask about the records held about you in Plait, contact your care provider first. We will help them answer your request.

## Cookies

**This website does not set cookies.** If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers that choice on your own device. It is never sent to us.

Our introductory video is hosted on YouTube. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. It then plays in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

## Who we share data with

We never sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers named in this policy, so that they can provide their services to us, or where the law requires us to.

## Keeping data secure

We are certified to ISO 27001 (information security) and Cyber Essentials Plus, and our NHS Data Security and Protection Toolkit assessment exceeds the required standards.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you
- have inaccurate data corrected
- have your data deleted
- object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your data
- receive your data in a portable format
- withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email [contact-now@reallycare.org](mailto:contact-now@reallycare.org). There is no charge, and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
